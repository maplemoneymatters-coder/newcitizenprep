Canadian citizenship test guide

What to expect and how to prepare.

The test at a glance

ItemDetails
Who writes itAdults aged 18 to 54 (applicants 55+ are exempt)
FormatUsually written, 20 questions (multiple choice and true/false); may be an interview
Time45 minutes
Pass mark15 out of 20 correct
SourceAll questions are based on the official Discover Canada study guide

Topics on the test

  • Rights and responsibilities of citizenship
  • Canadian history
  • Modern Canada
  • How Canadians govern themselves and federal elections
  • The justice system
  • Canadian symbols
  • Canada's economy and regions

Study tips

  1. Read Discover Canada once, then use practice questions to find gaps.
  2. Learn the capitals, the three levels of government and key dates such as 1867 and 1982.
  3. Redo missed questions until you can explain the answer.
  4. Take at least two Quick Tests under timed conditions.

Always check the latest rules and the current study guide on the official IRCC website; details can change.
